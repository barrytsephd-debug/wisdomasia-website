# Visual asset provenance

The final website retains the original WisdomAsia and WIN logos. It uses no photographs or artwork extracted from the corporate PDF. Decorative red-orange accents are CSS.

All three photographic assets are AI-generated illustrative scenes, created with the built-in image generation tool. They do not document actual clients, staff, fieldwork or identifiable locations. Alt text identifies the scenes as illustrative. Original PNG outputs are preserved outside the checkout; web-optimised WebP versions are under `public/assets/`.

## chinese-urban-life.webp — hero

Final prompt: Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide 16:9 website hero background for WisdomAsia, an established independent market research consultancy. Create a convincing editorial photograph of everyday modern Chinese urban commercial life in an anonymous mainland Chinese city. Street-level view diagonally along a lively pedestrian shopping street at blue hour, contemporary glass commercial buildings mixed with ordinary storefronts and restrained Chinese signage in the background, diverse adult Chinese pedestrians in candid slight motion blur, warm shop-window light and subtle red-orange accents. Human consumer life and commerce, not tourism. Composition: strongest interesting activity across the centre and right, the left third darker and visually quieter for HTML headline overlay. Wide realistic documentary photograph, sophisticated natural colours, inviting and credible, no dystopia or cyberpunk, no excessive neon. No recognisable skyline, specific city landmark, identifiable brands, Japanese text, torii, pagodas, flags, dragons, lantern decorations or tourist clichés. No overlaid headline, no logo, no watermark. This is an illustrative anonymous Chinese city, not an identifiable real location.
