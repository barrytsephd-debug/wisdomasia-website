01 / About WisdomAsia

An independent mind.
An informed perspective.

Established in 2004, WisdomAsia is an independent market research consultancy specialising in consumer, brand and communications research, with particular expertise in China.

We draw on international research experience and trusted professional relationships to shape each assignment around the question at hand. Our work connects local market understanding with the wider perspective organisations need to make informed decisions.

Our longstanding philosophy, Transcultural Intelligence, is simple: research becomes more valuable when findings are understood in their cultural context.

Our philosophy. Our reason for being.

Transcultural
Intelligence

Data can tell us what people say and do. Cultural understanding helps us interpret what it means.

Language, values, social expectations and everyday experience shape how people understand brands and respond to communications. The same message can carry different meanings across markets.

We bring research evidence and cultural context together, translating local understanding into insight that organisations can use. This is the thinking behind WisdomAsia, and the meaning of our longstanding tagline.

How this shapes our work ↗

Three decades in research.
A career across cultures.

Barry founded WisdomAsia in 2004, following research roles at Nielsen and Synovate and client-side experience with Nokia. His work spans Hong Kong, Singapore and mainland China, with particular depth in qualitative research, consumer understanding, and brand and communications research.

He holds a PhD in Psychology and lectures in psychology. This combination of commercial experience, cultural understanding and psychological knowledge informs WisdomAsia’s approach to interpreting what people say, how they respond and what their choices mean.
